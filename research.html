Research

Research in sport science

My work is in exercise physiology applied to team sports, and to basketball in particular: the physical and energetic demands placed on players and referees, the validity and reliability of field tests, and the link between physical fitness and in-game performance. The aim is to produce knowledge that coaches and conditioning staff can use.

Research topics

Demands of basketball

Physical and physiological load in male and female players, youth and elite.

Basketball refereeing

Aerobic and anaerobic load, energy expenditure and field testing of referees.

Field-test validity

Reliability and validity of the Illinois agility test, the repeated agility test and the Yo-Yo IR1.

Load and recovery

Monitoring with heart rate and heart-rate variability.
