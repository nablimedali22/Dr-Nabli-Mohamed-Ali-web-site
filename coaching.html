Coaching

Strength and conditioning built on science

Every programme starts with an assessment: field tests, heart-rate monitoring, jump and speed measurement, and blood lactate where it is useful. These are the same measurements I use in research. They let me build a precise plan, track training load and adjust week by week. You get a measured programme, explained to you and matched to your sport, your level and your calendar.

Who I work with

Clubs and teams

Season-long conditioning, training camps and training-load monitoring.

Team-sport athletes

Individual programmes, in person in Doha or online.

Individuals

General fitness, body-weight management and return to training.

Services

All services are quoted on request, based on your goals, your schedule and the length of the engagement.

  • Performance assessment

    A full physical evaluation with a written report.

  • One-to-one coaching

    A tailored programme with regular follow-up, in Doha.

  • Online coaching

    Programming, remote monitoring and weekly adjustments, anywhere in the world.

  • Team and club conditioning

    A full season or a single training camp.

  • Nutrition guidance

    Eating and body-weight advice built around your training.
